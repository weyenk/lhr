I love a good egg roll, but I don't love heating up a pot of oil on a random Tuesday just to get my fix. This is the shortcut I make instead — all the same flavors packed into the filling of an egg roll, none of the wrapping or frying, on the table in about 25 minutes flat.

It started as a way to use up half a bag of coleslaw mix that was about to go bad in my fridge, and it's turned into one of the recipes I come back to most. Ground turkey keeps it light, the cabbage and carrots stay crisp instead of going mushy, and that drizzle of spicy mayo on top does the same job the dipping sauce would at a restaurant — except now it's built right into the bowl.

**Pro Tip:** Don't walk away from the skillet once the coleslaw mix goes in. It only needs 4-5 minutes to wilt down — any longer and you lose the crunch that makes this taste like the inside of an egg roll instead of a plain stir-fry.

## Egg Roll in a Bowl

**Tips & Variations:**

- Ground pork is the more traditional choice here if you want something richer than turkey or chicken.
- No coleslaw mix on hand? A bag of shredded cabbage plus a couple of shredded carrots works just as well.
- Serve it over rice or cauliflower rice to stretch it into a bigger meal.

However you dress it up, save a little extra drizzle for the top — that first bite with the sauce still cold against the hot filling is the whole point.
