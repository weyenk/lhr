sections:
  - heading: "When the Day Wins"
    body: "You know the kind of day I mean. The one that starts early, runs long, and leaves you standing in front of the fridge at seven o'clock wondering if cereal counts as dinner. I've had more of those lately than I'd like to admit, and on those nights I want two things that don't usually go together: something comforting enough to feel like a hug, and something light enough that I don't feel weighed down afterward. These burritos are how I get both, and they're on the table in about 30 minutes."
  - heading: "Comfort Without the Heaviness"
    body: "A burrito is comfort food by definition. It's warm, it's filling, and it's wrapped up like a little present to yourself. But smothered burritos can easily tip into heavy territory, all melted cheese and rich red sauce. So I went the other direction. Instead of a blanket of cheese, these get a spoonful of a creamy green cilantro sauce that's cool, bright, and a little tangy. It soaks into the golden tortilla just enough to feel indulgent while keeping every bite tasting fresh."
  - heading: "Thirty Minutes, Start to Plate"
    body: "The whole point of this meal is that it doesn't ask much of you. The filling comes together in one skillet while the sauce gets a quick whirl in the blender. Roll, toast the burritos in a hot pan until the outsides turn golden, spoon the sauce over the top, and you're done. No oven preheating, no marinating, no sink full of dishes waiting for you afterward. On a night when my energy is gone, that matters just as much as how it tastes."
  - heading: "The Finishing Touches"
    body: "This is where tired-me still gets to have a little fun. A few slices of ripe avocado fanned out on the side, a couple of dots and swipes of smoky red chili sauce for heat and color, and a sprig of fresh cilantro on top of each burrito. It takes about thirty seconds, but it turns a quick weeknight dinner into something that looks like it came from a restaurant. And honestly, sitting down to a plate that looks this pretty makes a long day feel a little more worth it."
  - heading: "Why This One Stays in the Rotation"
    body: "I come back to this meal because it meets me where I am. It's fast when I'm short on time, fresh when I'm craving something bright, and cozy when I just need dinner to take care of me for a change. If your week is looking anything like mine, keep a pack of tortillas and a bunch of cilantro in the kitchen. Future you, standing in front of the fridge at seven o'clock, will be very grateful."
