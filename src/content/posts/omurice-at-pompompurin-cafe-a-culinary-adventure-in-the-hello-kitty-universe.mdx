steps:
  - "Heat butter in a large skillet over medium heat."
  - "Add onion and garlic and cook until softened, about 5 minutes."
  - "Stir in cooked rice, peas, and corn. Cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring constantly, until heated through."
  - "Season with soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce, salt, and pepper."
  - "In a medium bowl, whisk together eggs and milk. Season with salt and pepper."
  - "Heat 1 tablespoon butter in the same skillet over medium heat."
  - "Pour the egg mixture into the skillet and cook, stirring occasionally, until almost set."
  - "Gently lift the edges of the omelette with a spatula to allow uncooked egg to flow underneath."
  - "Continue cooking until the omelette is set but still slightly moist."
  - "Carefully flip the omelette over the fried rice."
  - "Serve immediately, garnished with ketchup and chopped green onions, if desired."
