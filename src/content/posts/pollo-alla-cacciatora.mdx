steps:
  - "Prep the chicken: pat the chicken dry. If the breasts are thick, slice them in half horizontally so they cook evenly. Season both sides with half the salt and all the pepper."
  - "Sear: heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the chicken 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden, then set it aside on a plate. It will finish cooking later."
  - "Sauté the onion and garlic: lower the heat to medium. Add the onion and cook about 5 minutes until soft and lightly golden. Stir in the garlic for 30 seconds, until fragrant."
  - "Add the broth and lemon: pour in the broth and lemon juice together, scraping up the browned bits from the pan."

Classic cacciatora, the "hunter's style" chicken, is usually a slow, rustic braise of bone-in pieces, peppers, and wine. This is my lighter, weeknight version. Boneless chicken breast gets a quick sear, then finishes in a bright, lemony broth with cherry tomatoes that burst into a sauce on their own. It's all done in one skillet in about 35 minutes.

![An overhead view of a seared chicken breast piled with burst cherry tomatoes and soft onions, crowned with fresh basil, on a speckled teal plate](https://loveheatrelationship.com/images/posts/pollo-alla-cacciatora/plated.jpg)

**Pro Tip:** Don't skip scraping up the browned bits when the broth and lemon go in. That fond from searing the chicken is where most of the flavor in the pan sauce comes from.

## Pollo alla Cacciatora

**To Serve:**

Spoon the tomatoes and pan sauce over the chicken. It's great with crusty bread, creamy polenta, or pasta to catch the sauce.
